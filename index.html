    You do not manage us. You do not translate for us. You simply arrive at the mirror, and the face looking back is yours, only ready for every camera in the room.

    01

    Two decades, two continents

    Nikoleta trained in London from 2009: five years inside fashion, advertising, editorial and celebrity beauty before relocating to Barcelona in 2013 to build a bridal team of her own. That production discipline, the kind that has to survive a flash, a deadline and a client who cannot be kept waiting, is still the foundation of every wedding morning today.

    Our destination portfolio includes private and VIP celebrations across Europe, and international travel with clients such as Huda Al Khamis-Kanoo, working alongside her at events across multiple countries.

    02

    A makeup artist who is also behind the camera

    Nikoleta is also a photographer, working in portrait and boudoir photography. That double perspective changes how a bridal look is built: not just what looks beautiful in the mirror, but what actually reads on a sensor, under a flash, in harsh midday sun or low golden-hour light.

    Knowing how a lens sees skin, how contour holds up in profile, how a highlight either lifts a cheekbone in a photograph or simply disappears: that is not something a makeup artist learns from the other side of the camera. It comes from having stood behind it, framing women at their most photographed, most photogenic, most themselves.

    It is the same eye we bring to your wedding morning: a look composed not only for the room, but for every photograph that will outlive it.

    03

    One team, every language your family speaks

    International weddings mean international families. Our team communicates across several languages, so nothing gets lost between the bride, her mother, the planner and the photographer on the one morning none of them can repeat.

    We are equally at home with:

    • Bridal hair & makeup
    • Bridesmaids, mothers of the bride and groom, flower girls
    • Groom grooming
    • Welcome and rehearsal dinners, engagement shoots, civil ceremonies
    • Multi-day celebrations, including our dedicated Indian wedding experience
    • On-location touch-ups through the ceremony, portraits and reception

    Large bridal party or intimate elopement: the schedule scales, the standard does not.

    The only thing that travels with you is calm

    Every look begins the same way regardless of the postcode: your dress, your venue's light, the local weather, the culture of your celebration and the story you want your photographs to tell in ten years. Destination changes the packing list. It never changes the attention.
